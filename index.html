How I work

Listen. Design. Deploy. Enable.

A practical process that starts with real operational needs and carries through to a solution people can rely on and build upon.

  1. Listen

    Identify key stakeholders and capture core business objectives, both immediate operational needs and long-term strategic goals. Understand the day-to-day environment to pinpoint where efficiency, clarity, or confidence is being lost.

  2. Design

    Architect solutions closely aligned with business goals, operational constraints, and long-term sustainability. Shape a straightforward, repeatable blueprint that directly solves core challenges while scaling seamlessly as needs evolve.

  3. Deploy

    Validate through structured testing and stakeholder acceptance to ensure seamless execution, high reliability, and strict alignment with business goals.

  4. Enable

    Operationalize systems through effective knowledge transfer, team empowerment, and continuous feedback loops. Support long-term success by maintaining high visibility and refining workflows as operational and business priorities shift.

Standards & Documentation

DWDM standards and procedures guide field and provisioning teams through consistent, secure network deployment and maintenance.

DWDM
  • Commissioning: Configuration and Standards Guide — Guides field technicians through loading the correct nodal code and applying secure, standardized configurations before deployment.
  • Equipment Installation and Node Insertion — Aligns field technicians and provisioners on fiber connections between nodes, then provides EMS standards to follow once a device is visible in the network.
  • Service Provisioning — Guides provisioning teams through turning up wave services across the optical network in accordance with established standards.
  • CBOA Nodal Software Upgrade via WSNOC — An end-to-end method of procedure (MOP) for upgrading 1830 PSS and PSD-10 shelves, with validation steps and checks to help protect the network's 99.999% availability objective.
